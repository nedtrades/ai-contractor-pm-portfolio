# Weekly Status Update (one page per site)

Use this for a weekly update to a Facilities Manager or Store Manager. Keep it to one screen. Copy the block below into the email body, not an attachment.

---

**Subject:** [Site name/number, City ST]: [scope], week [n] of [N] ([On schedule / At risk / Behind])

**Summary:** [One sentence on schedule status.] [One sentence naming the single thing you need from the reader, with a time, or "Nothing needed from you this week."]

**Done this week**
- [Completed item, with record or photo reference]
- [Completed item, with record or photo reference]

**Open items**
| Item | Status | Owner (role) | Due |
|---|---|---|---|
| | | | |

**Next week**
- [Planned work, with any access or shutdown it needs]

**Store or building impact**
- [Planned shutdowns, noise, closed areas, crane or roof access, with dates and times. "None" if none.]

**Decisions needed from you**
- [Decision, options, deadline, what happens if no decision. "None" if none.]

**Attachments:** [list]

[Signature: role, Falcon ICM, LLC]

---

### Rules

1. The ask goes in the first two lines.
2. Every "done" item points to a record.
3. Anything not confirmed is marked "pending" with who it is pending on.
4. Schedule status uses one of three words: On schedule, At risk, Behind. "At risk" and "Behind" need a reason and a recovery plan.
5. No percent-complete numbers unless they come from the schedule of values.
