# Client Correspondence Style Guide

This is the house style for every message Falcon ICM, LLC sends to a customer, whether I write it or an AI assistant drafts it for me. The AI prompts in [`../prompts/`](../prompts/) include this guide.

## 1. Order of information

1. **Status or answer first.** Is the store open? Is the water on? Is the job on schedule? Answer that in sentence one.
2. **The ask second.** What do you need from the reader, by when?
3. **Details after.** Facts, tables, attachments.
4. **Next step last**, with an owner role and a date.

## 2. Words

- Plain English. Write for a Store Manager, not a design engineer, unless the reader is a design engineer.
- Use trade terms when they are the right word, and explain them once: "T&P (temperature and pressure) relief valve."
- Name equipment by its tag and its plain name: "RTU-2 (rooftop unit 2, over the sales floor)."
- Use role names for people on the customer side: Store Manager, Facilities Manager.
- Short sentences. One idea per sentence.
- No sales language and no filler: no "leverage," "seamless," "robust," "cutting-edge," "going forward" as padding, "please do not hesitate."

## 3. Facts and claims

- Every number comes from a reading, a document or a quote. Cite the source.
- Say who observed what. "The Store Manager reports no product damage" is different from "no product damage."
- If something is unknown, say so and say when it will be known.
- Never promise energy savings, life expectancy, or performance results unless a document backs it and the document is cited.
- Never make a code interpretation the authority having jurisdiction has not made. Say "subject to inspector approval" when that is the case.

## 4. Times and dates

- Give day and time: "Thursday 7:00 AM," not "later this week."
- Use the site's local time zone. Say so when the reader is in another zone.
- For a commitment you might miss, give the time you will report back if it slips.

## 5. Format

- Subject line: site, scope, status.
- Tables for open items, options and timelines.
- Photos are numbered and referenced in the text (Photo 03-2).
- Attachments carry document IDs from the [document register](document-register.csv).

## 6. Tone

- Calm and specific, especially in incident notices.
- Own mistakes in one sentence, then give the fix: "We left the roof hatch unlocked Tuesday. It is now locked, and the last person off the roof will check it each day."
- No blame of other trades in writing to the customer. State the condition and the fix.

## 7. Review before sending

Run the [human verification checklist](../prompts/00-human-verification-checklist.md). A licensed professional approves every message that states a fact about plumbing or HVAC/R work.
