# Spec-to-Service Record

| Document ID | Revision | Site | Asset / system tag | Date |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| FM-855-04 | Rev A | Retail Store #____, ________ | ______ | ______ |

**Purpose.** A vendor-neutral record that locks in serviceability decisions during design and submittal review, records commissioning baselines, and gives a monthly signed log that the agreed service levels were met. It is the starting point for the turnover checklist and for warranty and service work later. It does not depend on any manufacturer's dashboard.

**Scope (check all that apply):** ☐ Rooftop unit ☐ Split system ☐ Boiler ☐ Hydronic loop ☐ Ground-source heat pump / loop ☐ Pump / circulator ☐ Heat exchanger ☐ Domestic water heater ☐ Backflow assembly ☐ Other: ________
