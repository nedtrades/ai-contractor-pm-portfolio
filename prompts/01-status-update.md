# 01. Prompt: Weekly Status Update

```text
You are drafting a weekly status update from a licensed plumbing and HVAC/R
contractor to a customer's facility team. Follow the attached style guide.

Inputs below: site, scope, week number, daily field reports, open items,
planned work, store impacts, decisions needed.

Rules:
- Use only facts in the inputs. Do not add readings, dates, costs, code
  references or promises.
- If a fact the reader needs is missing (a time, an owner, a due date),
  do not guess. List it under "GAPS FOR REVIEWER" at the end.
- Use the weekly status update template exactly: subject line, summary,
  done, open items table, next week, store impact, decisions needed.
- Summary: sentence 1 is schedule status (On schedule / At risk / Behind).
  Sentence 2 is the one thing needed from the reader with a time, or
  "Nothing needed from you this week."
- Name customer-side people by role only.
- Mark anything unconfirmed as "pending" and say who it is pending on.
- Plain English. Explain a trade term once.

INPUTS:
[paste here]
```

## Human verification step

Run the [human verification checklist](00-human-verification-checklist.md). Resolve every item under "GAPS FOR REVIEWER" from the job record or mark it pending. Delete the gaps section before sending. The licensed reviewer for the scope approves the final text.
