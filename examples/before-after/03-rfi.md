# 3. Request for information (RFI)

**Illustrative rewrite. Not a real RFI. All data is an illustrative sample.**

Scenario: a store remodel drawing shows a new hand sink where the existing waste line is too high to drain it.

## Before

> RFI - sink in back room can't drain, waste is too high. what do you want to do?

## After

> **RFI #07 (Retail Store #0000 remodel): new hand sink HS-2 cannot drain to the existing waste line as drawn**
>
> **Drawing / spec reference:** Plumbing plan sheet P-101, fixture HS-2 (back room).
>
> **Field condition:** The existing waste line at the HS-2 location sits higher than the sink's trap outlet would need to be to drain by gravity. Photos 07-1 to 07-3 attached, with tape measure in view.
>
> **Question:** How should HS-2 be drained?
>
> **Options we see (for the design team to decide):**
> 1. Move HS-2 to the location noted in the attached markup, where it can tie into the waste line by gravity.
> 2. Keep the location and add a listed under-sink drain pump, if allowed for this fixture by the design team and the local authority.
> 3. Keep the location and run a new waste line below the slab to the nearest suitable connection (requires slab cutting).
>
> **Impact if not answered by Friday:** HS-2 rough-in stops. Other rough-in continues. Fixture set for the back room would move out by the number of days the answer is late.
>
> **Requested response by:** Friday, 12:00 PM.
>
> Submitted by: Licensed Plumbing Manager, Falcon ICM, LLC

## What changed

- **The RFI cites the exact sheet and fixture tag.** The design team does not have to call to ask which sink.
- **Field condition and question are separate.** The condition is a fact with photo evidence; the question is one sentence.
- **Options are offered, but the decision stays with the design team.** The contractor does not make a design call in an RFI.
- **Option 2 is conditional on approval.** The AI draft first listed it without that condition; I added it in review, because the contractor does not decide what the design team and authority will accept.
- **The schedule impact is specific and limited** to what actually stops.
