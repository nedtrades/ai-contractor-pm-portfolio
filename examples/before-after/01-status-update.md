# 1. Weekly status update to a Facilities Manager

**Illustrative rewrite. Not a real client message. All data is an illustrative sample.**

Scenario: rooftop unit replacement and domestic water heater replacement at one store, week 2 of 3.

## Before

> Subject: store update
>
> hey, RTU-2 set Mon, crane went fine. RTU-3 next wk. WH done but T&P valve was busted in the box so temp one on. gas inspector came, passed. need someone to unlock roof hatch wed. thx

## After

> **Subject: Retail Store #0000, Anytown OR: HVAC and water heater work, week 2 of 3 (on schedule)**
>
> **Summary:** On schedule. One request for you: please have the roof hatch unlocked by 7:00 AM Wednesday.
>
> **Done this week**
> - RTU-2 set on the roof Monday morning, before store opening, using the crane window approved by the Store Manager. Unit is wired, piped and running. Start-up readings are in the attached start-up sheet.
> - Gas piping to RTU-2 passed the gas inspection Tuesday. Inspection record attached.
> - Domestic water heater replaced. The store has had hot water since Tuesday 11:00 AM.
>
> **Open items**
> | Item | Status | Owner (role) | Due |
> |---|---|---|---|
> | Water heater T&P relief valve | Valve shipped with the unit was damaged. A listed temporary valve is installed and the heater is safe to run. Replacement is on order. | Licensed Plumbing Manager | Thursday |
> | Roof hatch access | Needed for RTU-3 prep | Store Manager | Wednesday 7:00 AM |
>
> **Next week:** RTU-3 set (crane window requested for Monday before opening; confirmation pending from the Store Manager), then start-up and balancing.
>
> **Store impact:** No planned shutdowns this week. RTU-3 changeover will take one zone's cooling offline for about one business day; we will confirm the date with the Store Manager at least two days ahead.
>
> **Decisions needed from you:** None this week.

## What changed

- **Subject line names the site, the work and the schedule status.** A Facilities Manager with 40 open jobs can sort it without opening it.
- **The one ask is in the first two lines**, with a time. In the "before" it was buried at the end with no owner.
- **"Busted T&P" became a safety statement.** The reader's real question is "is it safe?" The after answers it, and says who closes it and when.
- **Every "done" item points to a record** (start-up sheet, inspection record). The facts are the same; now they can be checked.
- **Store impact gets its own line.** Store operations care about downtime more than equipment tags.
- **Removed nothing true and added nothing unverified.** The crane confirmation is marked "pending," because it was.
