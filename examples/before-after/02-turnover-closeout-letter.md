# 2. Turnover and closeout letter

**Illustrative rewrite. Not a real client message. All data is an illustrative sample.**

Scenario: hydronic heating boiler and pump replacement at a commercial building, work complete, handing over to the owner's facilities team.

## Before

> Job's done. Boiler and pumps in and running. Manuals are in the mech room. Warranty is 1 yr labor. Invoice to follow. Call if any issues.

## After

> **Subject: Turnover: Commercial Building #0000, Anytown OR: boiler and hydronic pump replacement (Project #0000)**
>
> The boiler and hydronic pump replacement is complete. The system has been running on its normal schedule since start-up on the date shown in the commissioning report. This letter lists what you are receiving, what is still open, and how warranty works.
>
> **1. What you are receiving**
> | Item | Where it is |
> |---|---|
> | Commissioning report with start-up readings (supply/return temps, pump differential pressure, combustion test) | Attached, document COM-0000 Rev A |
> | Equipment list with make, model and serial numbers | Attached, Appendix A |
> | Manufacturer manuals and warranty registrations | Binder in the mechanical room and PDF set attached |
> | As-built piping sketch showing new valves and isolation points | Attached, Appendix B |
> | Permit and final inspection record | Attached, Appendix C |
> | Training sign-in for the owner's maintenance staff | Attached, Appendix D |
>
> **2. Open items (punch list)**
> | # | Item | Owner (role) | Target date |
> |---|---|---|---|
> | 1 | Replace one missing valve tag on the pump B return | Field Technician | Next scheduled visit |
> | 2 | Confirm BMS alarm for boiler lockout reaches the owner's on-call line | Controls contractor with Facilities Manager | Pending a test date from the controls contractor |
>
> **3. Warranty**
> - Our workmanship warranty: one year from the turnover date on this letter.
> - Equipment warranties are the manufacturers'. Registration confirmations are in Appendix A.
> - To report a problem, use the service line in our signature. Please include the building number and equipment tag.
>
> **4. What we recommend for the first year**
> - Check the hydronic system's fill pressure gauge monthly against the value recorded in the commissioning report.
> - Schedule the first annual boiler service before the next heating season.
>
> **Sign-off:** Please countersign the attached turnover acceptance form, or reply with any item you consider incomplete. Turnover is accepted when the form is signed.
>
> Licensed Plumbing Manager / Mechanical Manager, Falcon ICM, LLC

## What changed

- **"Manuals are in the mech room" became an indexed list** of everything handed over, with a document ID for each. The owner can check completeness without walking the building.
- **Open items are stated, not hidden.** A closeout that admits two small open items, with owners and dates, is more credible than "job's done."
- **Warranty terms are spelled out**, including the difference between our workmanship warranty and the manufacturers' equipment warranties, and how to file a claim.
- **The owner knows when turnover is accepted:** when the form is signed. That ends arguments about when warranty started.
- **First-year recommendations are routine maintenance only.** No promised savings or performance figures.
