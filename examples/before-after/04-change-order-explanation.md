# 4. Change-order explanation for a non-technical approver

**Illustrative rewrite. Not a real change order. All amounts and quantities are illustrative samples.**

Scenario: hidden corroded galvanized pipe found when a restroom wall is opened.

## Before

> CO #3 - found galv line in wall rotted out, need to replace approx 40 ft + fittings. T&M. Add $X and 1 day. Pls approve asap.

## After

> **Change Order #3, Retail Store #0000 restroom remodel: replace corroded galvanized water line inside the opened wall**
>
> **What we found:** When we opened the restroom wall for the new fixtures, we found about 40 feet of original galvanized water pipe with heavy corrosion at the threaded joints. Photos 03-1 to 03-4 attached.
>
> **Why it matters to you:** The new fixtures connect to this pipe. If we connect to it as-is and a joint fails later, the leak would be inside a finished wall. Fixing it then means opening the wall again and closing the restroom again.
>
> **Your options**
> | | Option A: replace now (recommended) | Option B: connect to existing pipe |
> |---|---|---|
> | Added cost | $X (fixed price, see attached breakdown) | $0 now |
> | Added schedule | 1 working day | None |
> | Restroom downtime | Already closed for the remodel; adds 1 day | None added now |
> | Risk after turnover | The corroded section is removed | The corroded section stays; condition is noted in the turnover record |
>
> **My recommendation:** Option A, while the wall is open.
>
> **Decision needed by:** Friday 12:00 PM, to keep the current finish date. If we do not hear back, we will close the wall with Option B and record the condition, so the store is not held up.
>
> Prepared by: Licensed Plumbing Manager, Falcon ICM, LLC

## What changed

- **"Rotted out" became a description with photos.** The approver can see it.
- **"Why it matters" is written for a person who does not read pipe.** It explains the risk in terms of a future wall opening and restroom closure.
- **Both options are shown side by side**, including the cheap one. The approver decides with the full picture.
- **"T&M, add $X" became a fixed price with a breakdown.** The amount itself is left as $X here because this is a sample. No dollar figure is made up.
- **The default if nobody answers is stated.** That protects the store's schedule and the contractor's record.
