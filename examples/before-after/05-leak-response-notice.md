# 5. Incident and leak-response notice

**Illustrative rewrite. Not a real incident. All times and details are illustrative samples.**

Scenario: an after-hours call for water on the floor at a store.

## Before

> Had a leak at the store last night. Supply line on sink blew. Fixed it. Floor got wet.

## After

> **Subject: Leak at Retail Store #0000, Anytown OR: resolved, store open, one follow-up visit Thursday**
>
> **Status:** Resolved. Water is off at one hand sink only. The rest of the store has water. The store opened on time.
>
> **Timeline (all times local)**
> | Time | Event |
> |---|---|
> | 5:40 AM | Store Manager reports water on the floor in the back-room restroom |
> | 5:45 AM | On-call dispatch confirms; Store Manager is talked through closing the sink's isolation valve |
> | 6:25 AM | Field Technician arrives |
> | 6:40 AM | Cause found: failed braided supply line at hand sink HS-1 |
> | 7:05 AM | Supply line replaced, no further leaking after a 15-minute observation |
>
> **Affected area:** Back-room restroom and part of the hallway. Store staff cleaned the floor. The Store Manager reports no product damage. We did not inspect for water under the flooring; see next steps.
>
> **Cause:** The supply line failed. It was the same age as the other supply lines in this restroom. The failed line is bagged and held for the facility team if wanted.
>
> **Next steps**
> | # | Action | Owner (role) | When |
> |---|---|---|---|
> | 1 | Replace the other supply lines in this restroom (same age) | Field Technician | Thursday, before opening |
> | 2 | Moisture check of the hallway floor and the wall base | Field Technician | Thursday |
> | 3 | Decide if supply lines at other stores of the same build year should be checked | Facilities Manager | At your discretion |
>
> **Photos:** 6 attached (before, cause, repair, area).
>
> Licensed Plumbing Manager, Falcon ICM, LLC

## What changed

- **Status first.** A Store Manager or district lead reading at 7 AM needs to know if the store is open and if water is on.
- **Timeline with times.** This becomes the incident record for the facility team and any insurance question later.
- **Clear line between what we know and what we did not check** ("We did not inspect for water under the flooring"). The AI's first draft said "no damage"; I changed it in review because nobody verified that. The Store Manager's report is attributed to the Store Manager.
- **The multi-site question is raised, not decided.** Whether to check other stores is the Facilities Manager's call. The notice gives them the information to make it.
- **The failed part is kept** in case the facility team or an insurer wants it.
