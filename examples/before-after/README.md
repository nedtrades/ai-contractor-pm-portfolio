# Before and after: contractor notes vs. AI-assisted, human-reviewed messages

**Illustrative rewrites.** Every pair below was written to show the method. None of them is a quote from a real client, a real job or a real message. Sites, times, readings and amounts are illustrative samples.

Each file has three parts:

1. **Before:** the kind of note a busy contractor sends from the truck.
2. **After:** the same facts, drafted with an AI assistant from my field notes and then checked line by line by the licensed professional before sending.
3. **What changed:** the specific edits and why they matter to the person reading it.

The "after" never adds a fact that was not in the field notes. When the "before" was missing a fact the customer needs (a time, a next step, who decides), the AI flags the gap and I fill it from the job record, or the message says it is pending.
